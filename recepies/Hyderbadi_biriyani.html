Hyderbadi Biriyani

Description

Hyderabadi biryani is a style of biryani originating from Hyderabad, India made with basmati rice and meat.

Ingridient

  • Basmati rice – 2 cups
  • Chicken or mutton – 500g
  • Yogrut – 1 cup
  • Onions – 2 large
  • Ginger-garlic paste – 2 tbsp
  • Green chilies – 3–4
  • Mint & coriander leaves – 1 cup
  • Spices – garam masala, turmeric, red chili powder, salt
  • Whole spices – cloves, cardamom, cinnamon
  • Ghee/oil – 4 tbsp
  • Saffron – a few strands soaked in warm milk

Steps

  1. Mix chicken/mutton with yogurt, ginger-garlic paste, chili powder, turmeric, garam masala, fried onions, and salt. Let it rest.
  2. Boil rice with salt and whole spices until 70% cooked. Drain and keep aside.
  3. Spread marinated meat at the bottom.Add chopped herbs, green chilies, some fried onions.Layer half-cooked rice on top.Sprinkle saffron milk and ghee.
  4. Seal pot with a lid or dough.Cook on high heat for 5 mins, then on low for 25–30 mins.
  5. Gently mix before serving. Serve with raita or salan.
