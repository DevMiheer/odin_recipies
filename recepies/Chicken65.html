Chicken65

Description

Chicken 65 is a spicy, deep-fried South Indian chicken starter made with marinated chicken, curry leaves, and bold spices. It's crispy, flavorful, and served hot as an appetizer or snack.

Ingridient

  • 500g Boneless chicken (cut into small pieces)
  • 2 tbsp Cornflour
  • 2 tbsp All-purpose flour
  • 1 tbsp Ginger-garlic paste
  • 1 tsp Red chili powder
  • ½ tsp Turmeric powder
  • 1 tsp Garam masala
  • 1 tbsp Lemon juice or vinegar
  • Salt to taste
  • Oil for deep frying
  • Few Curry leaves
  • 1–2 Green chilies

Steps

  1. Mix chicken with flour, spices, ginger-garlic paste, lemon juice, and salt. Let it rest for 30 mins.
  2. Heat oil and deep-fry the chicken pieces until golden and crisp. Drain on paper towels.
  3. In a small pan, heat a little oil, add curry leaves and green chilies, and sauté briefly.
  4. Add the fried chicken to the tempering and toss well.
  5. Enjoy hot with onion rings and lemon wedges.
