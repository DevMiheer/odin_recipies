Mushroom Do Pyaza

Description

Mushroom Do Pyaza is a savory and spicy dish where mushrooms are cooked with a generous amount of onions. The use of both sautéed and raw onions adds a depth of flavor, making it a popular choice in North Indian cuisine. It pairs wonderfully with naan or rice.

Ingredients

  • 200g Mushrooms
  • 2 Large Onions
  • 1 Tomato
  • 1 tsp Ginger-garlic paste
  • 1 tsp Cumin seeds
  • 1 tsp Red chili powder
  • 1 tsp Coriander powder
  • 1 tsp Garam masala
  • 1 tsp Turmeric powder
  • 2 Green chilies (slit)
  • 2 tbsp Oil
  • Fresh coriander
  • Salt to taste
  • Water

Steps

  1. Heat oil in a pan and add cumin seeds. Let them splutter. Add sliced onions (reserve some for garnish) and sauté until they turn golden brown.
  2. Add ginger-garlic paste and sauté until the raw smell disappears.
  3. Add pureed tomatoes, red chili powder, turmeric, coriander powder, and salt. Cook until the oil separates from the masala.
  4. Add sliced mushrooms to the pan. Stir and cook for 5–7 minutes until the mushrooms release their water and become tender.
  5. Add a little water to create a thicker gravy. Simmer for another 5–7 minutes to allow the mushrooms to absorb the flavors.
  6. Sprinkle garam masala and add the reserved sliced onions. Stir well and cook for another 2–3 minutes.
  7. Garnish with fresh coriander leaves and serve hot with naan, roti, or rice.
