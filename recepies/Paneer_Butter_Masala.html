Paneer Butter Masala

Description

Paneer Butter Masala is a rich and creamy North Indian curry made with paneer (Indian cottage cheese), simmered in a buttery tomato-based gravy. It's mildly spiced, slightly sweet, and perfect with naan or basmati rice. Loved for its smooth texture and aromatic flavors, it's a favorite in Indian restaurants.

Ingridient

  • 250g Paneer (cubed)
  • 2 tbsp Butter
  • 1 tbsp Oil
  • 2 Onions (chopped)
  • 3 Tomatoes (pureed)
  • 1 tbsp Ginger-Garlic Paste
  • 10 Cashews (soaked and blended)
  • 2 tbsp Fresh Cream
  • 1 tsp Garam Masala
  • 1 tsp Red Chili Powder
  • ¼ tsp Turmeric
  • Salt to taste
  • 1 tsp Kasuri Methi (dry fenugreek)
  • Fresh Coriander for garnish

Steps

  1. Heat oil and butter. Sauté onions till golden.
  2. Add ginger-garlic paste and cook briefly.
  3. Cook until oil separates.
  4. Add chili, turmeric, salt, and cashew paste. Cook 5–7 mins.
  5. Mix in paneer cubes gently.
  6. Add cream, garam masala, and kasuri methi. Simmer 2–3 mins.
  7. Garnish with coriander. Serve hot with naan or rice.
