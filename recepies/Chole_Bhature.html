Chole Bhature

Chole Bhature is a popular North Indian dish consisting of spicy chickpeas (chole) served with deep-fried bread (bhature). It's a hearty, flavorful dish enjoyed with onions, pickles, and yogurt.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup Chickpeas
  • 1 Onion
  • 2 Tomatoes
  • 1 tbsp Ginger-garlic paste
  • 1 Green chili (slit)
  • 1 tsp Garam masala
  • 1 tsp Red chili powder
  • 1 tsp Coriander powder
  • 1 tsp Cumin seeds
  • 1 Bay leaf
  • 1 tbsp Tea leaves (in a muslin cloth, optional for color)
  • Salt to taste
  • Water as required
  • 2 cups All-purpose flour (maida)
  • 1 tbsp Semolina (rava)
  • 1 tsp Baking powder
  • 1 tbsp Yogurt
  • Salt to taste
  • Water (to knead dough)
  • Oil (for frying)

Steps

  1. Pressure cook soaked chickpeas with water, bay leaf, and tea leaves until soft. Set aside.
  2. Heat oil in a pan, add cumin seeds, ginger-garlic paste, and sauté. Add chopped onions and cook until golden brown. Add tomato puree, chili powder, garam masala, and coriander powder. Cook for a few minutes.
  3. Add the cooked chickpeas to the gravy, add water to adjust consistency, and simmer for 10-15 minutes. Adjust salt and spices.
  4. Mix flour, semolina, baking powder, salt, and yogurt. Knead into a smooth dough using water. Cover and let it rest for 1 hour.
  5. Divide the dough into small balls, roll them into thick circles, and deep fry in hot oil until golden and puffed up.
  6. Serve hot chole with fresh, crispy bhature. Garnish with onions, coriander, and lemon wedges.
